FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know. Still have questions? Email us directly.

What exactly do you do?

We are book marketing and promotion specialists helping authors sell more books, engage readers, and build strong author brands through customized strategy. Services include book launches, ongoing promotion, Amazon optimization, social media, email marketing, reader engagement, author branding, and advertising campaigns.

Do you guarantee book sales?

We guarantee structured strategy and professional execution with transparent reporting and relentless optimization. Specific sales numbers cannot be guaranteed by any ethical marketer due to variables like book quality, market conditions, and timing, but we focus on measurable growth at every stage.

How is all communication handled?

Everything is handled via professional email. Every strategy, update, report, and decision is documented via email providing complete transparency, full accountability, and a permanent record of all work and communication.

Can you help an already published book?

Absolutely. Many authors come to us months or years after publication. It is never too late for professional marketing. We have successfully revived and reignited sales for books that had been sitting dormant for extended periods.

Can you help before my book is published?

Yes, and we strongly recommend starting early. Pre-launch marketing is one of the most powerful investments you can make to ensure a successful book release and strong opening momentum.

Who is Sue Strachan and how can I contact her?

Sue Strachan is an experienced publishing and literary professional who partners with Timothy Gabriel. She focuses on publishing direction and literary positioning. You can reach Sue directly at suestrachanbookmarketing@gmail.com for publishing-related inquiries.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing varies by scope, complexity, and duration. Solutions exist for various budget levels with clear scoping, no hidden fees, and fully documented agreements. Email timothygabriel.authorgrowth@gmail.com for a customized quote tailored to your situation.

Do you work with international authors?

Yes. All our work is conducted via email so geography is no barrier. We actively support authors from around the world across all time zones.

How quickly can you start?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 hours. Discovery begins immediately after initial contact and strategies are typically completed within one to two weeks. Time-sensitive launch projects can be expedited.

What makes you different from other marketers?

Exclusive specialization in books, completely custom strategies, full email documentation and accountability, focus on real results like sales and reviews rather than vanity metrics, long-term partnership commitment, selective client onboarding, and the added publishing expertise of Sue Strachan set us apart entirely.
